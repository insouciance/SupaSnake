# SUPA SNAKE — Character Visual Style Guide (owner-authored, 2026-08-07)

Authoritative visual target: the style-guide render (90s cartoon sheet).
"If the implementation technically satisfies these instructions but does not
visually resemble that character, it is not finished."

## Overall
Premium 3D character with the visual grammar of a bold 1990s cartoon/arcade
mascot. BOLD OUTLINES · CUBIC & CHUNKY · VIBRANT COLORS · CEL SHADING ·
PLAYFUL ENERGY. Genuinely 3D — never realistic, softly rendered, minimalist,
or ordinary beveled game-engine cubes.

## 1. Shape language — cubic & chunky
Body: true cubes (width = height = depth), thick, CLEARLY SEPARATED from
neighbors, rounded/beveled edges, substantial visible side faces, never
flattened into tiles or slabs. Head: large cubic volume, slightly oversized
(personality center), strong square silhouette, exaggerated features. Feels
like a physical collectible toy.

## 2. Cel shading — three-tone
HIGHLIGHT → MID TONE → SHADOW with relatively hard transitions. Clear light
direction; bright graphic highlights on top-facing edges; saturated midtones;
substantially darker shadow faces; high contrast. Never smooth PBR as the
dominant look; no muddy gradients.

## 3. Bold outlines
Thick near-black outline around the silhouette — visually substantial, never
a 1px technical edge. Thinner dark internal lines separate head features,
shades, braids, body cubes, material boundaries. Inked cartoon in 3D, not AO.

## 4. Palette — high saturation
Primary Supa orange/yellow-orange · secondary black/near-black · small white
details · highlights bright yellow-orange · shadows deep burnt orange/dark
warm. Local colors stay recognizable under every board theme (no heavy
environmental contamination, no metallic response, no excess reflections).

## 5. Shades — keep, oversized pixel style
Disproportionately large, thick black frames, angular/blocky, white
pixel-style reflections. A 90s attitude accessory, not eyewear.

## 6. Braids — keep, blocky
Chunky simplified block segments, near-black, strong upper-edge highlights,
cel faces, thick silhouette, small cubic orange/gold beads at the ends. They
frame the head and are part of the identity. Never realistic hair.

## 7. Face — minimal
Shades carry the expression. Minimal mouth, simple geometric treatment.

## 8. NO CAP
Remove the cap/headwear entirely; do not replace it. Top silhouette = cubic
head + braids.

## 9. Body segments
Same language as the head (outlines, top highlights, saturated mid, dark
shadow faces, rounded bevels, true cubic proportions) but simpler. The head
is the character; the body establishes rhythm. Each cube satisfying alone.

## 10. 90s energy
From rendering and shape language ONLY — exaggerated proportions, strong
silhouettes, angular graphic highlights, chunky contour, saturated color,
confidence. NO graffiti/speech bubbles/halftones/decorative-era graphics.

## 11. Never
Smooth PBR cubes · thin outlines · flattened segments · realistic hair or
sunglasses · soft Pixar rendering · pastels · excessive bloom ·
photorealistic reflections · cute-mobile over-rounding · voxel/pixel-art
treatment · flat sprites.

## Target read at gameplay distance
chunky → colorful → outlined → expressive → unmistakably Supa Snake.
Up close: polished enough that players rotate around what they own.
