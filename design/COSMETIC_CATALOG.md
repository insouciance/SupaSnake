## Accessibility Considerations

### Visual Accessibility

**Colorblind Modes:**
- Protanopia (red-blind): Shift reds to blue-green
- Deuteranopia (green-blind): Enhance red-blue contrast
- Tritanopia (blue-blind): Shift blues to reds
- Monochromacy: High-contrast patterns instead of colors

**High Contrast Mode:**
- All cosmetics have high-contrast variant
- Dynasty identification via shape/pattern, not just color
- Example: Ember = jagged edges, Crystal = smooth curves

**Reduced Motion Mode:**
- Animated cosmetics have static variant option
- Particle effects can be disabled
- Trail effects simplified to solid lines

### Performance Accessibility

**Low-End Device Mode:**
- All cosmetics have low-poly variant
- Particle effects reduced by 75%
- Shader complexity reduced to mobile-first

**Battery Saver Mode:**
- Epic/Legendary/Mythic effects downgraded to Rare quality
- 60fps cap reduced to 30fps
- Particle budget halved
